About the Mark

Why a labrador, why an aperture

The labrador in our mark stands for the way we work: loyal, focused, calm under pressure, and attentive to every detail of a project long after the excitement of the brief has faded. The six-bladed aperture beside it is a reminder that controlled light is controlled perception — that how a project is seen can be engineered with the same precision as how it's built. Together, they're the whole of what we do: steady devotion to your project, and the technical control to make it believable before it exists.
